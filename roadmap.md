# Build roadmap

- [x] 1. Authentication (Neon Auth), roles, multi-business data model and RLS.
- [x] 2. Inventory: products, categories, stock adjustments and history, edit/archive.
- [x] 2. Suppliers (list, create, edit, delete, link to products).
- [x] 2. Purchase orders and receiving stock: numbered orders, draft/ordered/received/cancelled, partial receipts, weighted-average cost, restock suggestions per supplier, WhatsApp/print to supplier.
- [x] Staff invites (email-bound, 7-day, single-use links) and team management (roles, deactivate).
- [x] Password reset by email.
- [x] 3. Sales and checkout (cash): barcode/search, discounts, printable and WhatsApp receipts, returns/refunds, retry-safe sales, stock updated atomically.
- [ ] 3b. Split payments, voids, tax/VAT settings, cashier discount limits.
- [x] 4. Customers: address book, purchase history, top spenders/frequent, credit limits and terms, credit sales (cash + credit split), credit ledger and statements, oldest-first repayments, overdue blocking, credit ageing, WhatsApp reminders, credit-aware refunds.
- [ ] 4b. Opening balances for existing debtors, write-offs, customer groups/discounts, loyalty points (ideas from NexoPOS/OpenTill).
- [ ] 5. Payments: cash, card, bank transfer, M-Pesa Daraja with reconciliation.
- [x] 6. Reports and analytics: daily/weekly/monthly sales, revenue, gross and net profit, expenses, top products with margins, salesperson performance, payment mix, stock valuation, fast/slow/dead stock, restock recommendations. Dashboard trend chart.
- [ ] 7. Product images, PWA install and offline sales with safe sync.
- [x] Cashier shifts and cash drawer: opening float, cash in/out with reasons, counted close with variance, automatic shift tagging of sales/refunds/repayments, optional "require a shift" rule, printable end-of-shift report.
